NP Membeship Benefits

Become a member of the Non Profit directory, to better serve the community.This membership includes a list of publishing benefits. Access to non profit networking events, and the power to request support from the community as well as other Non profits. No annual subscription.

Bronze Membership Benefits

Ideal option for start-up businesses. Bronze membership includes support from business professionals in the community with advertising opportunities and counsel. As well as, quarterly social events. Subscription fee for every 3 months.

Silver Membership Benefits

For serious business entrepreneurs looking to expand their target market. Silver membership includes all benefits from the bronze membership, plus an invitation to quarterly financial events with access to participate in newsletter every quarter. SUbscription fee for every 6 months

Gold Membership Benefits

The VIP option. Gold membership includes all benefits from bronze/silver membership plus additional support for social media platforms as well as the opportunity to participate in the monthly newsletter. Invitation to participate in seminars and luncheons when available. Subscription required for every 12 months.
